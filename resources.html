Navigator resource directory

Approved options for practical barriers.

A production directory would be maintained with the hospital and community partners. This prototype uses illustrative entries to demonstrate how a Navigator could search resources without browsing the open web during every patient interaction.

Prototype rule

These entries are examples, not current referrals or endorsements. A live program would verify availability, eligibility, language access, service area, and contact details before use.

Closed-loop habit

Do not stop at “resource provided.”

The Navigator workflow should record whether the patient could reach the resource, whether it fit the need, and whether the next step was completed or handed off.

01

Match

Choose an approved resource that fits the patient’s practical barrier.

02

Contact

Help the patient reach the resource using the preferred communication method.

03

Verify

Confirm whether the service is available and what the patient must do next.

04

Follow up

Check whether the practical service was actually arranged or accessed.

05

Close

Mark resolved, declined, escalated, or handed off instead of leaving an ambiguous referral.
