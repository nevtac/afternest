Hospital to home support

Going home should not mean figuring it out alone.

AfterNest is a proposed nonprofit that pairs older and vulnerable adults with trained Recovery Navigators for practical, non-clinical support after hospital discharge.

The gap we are addressing

The plan may be ready. Real life may not be.

Some patients leave the hospital knowing what should happen next but still face practical barriers that make follow-through difficult. AfterNest is designed for people who do not have enough reliable support at home to work through those barriers alone.

01 · Transportation

Can they get there?

A follow-up appointment is only useful if the patient has a realistic way to reach it.

02 · Technology

Can they use the tools?

Portals, video visits, digital scheduling, and device setup can become barriers after discharge.

03 · Navigation

Can they work through the process?

Language, caregiver, resource, and basic-needs barriers can make the next step harder to complete.

What support looks like

More than a reminder. Help with the barrier itself.

AfterNest is designed around practical follow-through. The Navigator stays involved until the approved task has a clear outcome, instead of simply sending another notification or resource list.

Transportation

Make the ride possible

Identify an appropriate transportation option, coordinate the logistics, and confirm that the plan is actually in place.

Technology

Make the tools usable

Help patients navigate portals, video visits, appointment details, and basic digital tasks without turning the program into another technology burden.

Transition support

Make the handoff clearer

Start before discharge so the patient knows who is helping with practical next steps once they return home.

How AfterNest works

One person stays with the practical problem until there is a next step.

The hospital keeps clinical ownership. AfterNest focuses on approved non-clinical barriers, with clear boundaries and a defined escalation path back to the healthcare team.
