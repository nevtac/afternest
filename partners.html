For hospital partners

A small, supervised pilot for practical barriers after discharge.

AfterNest is designed to complement an existing care-transition workflow, not replace it. The hospital keeps clinical ownership while trained Recovery Navigators take responsibility for approved non-clinical tasks such as transportation, technology, and resource navigation.

What the pilot funds

People and practical execution, not another reminder system.

Funding supports the workforce, supervision, transportation coordination, training, community-resource capacity, workflow technology, and evaluation required to make the support reliable.

Proposed pilot

Small enough to control. Structured enough to learn.

The academic model assumes one hospital partner, one clearly defined discharge population, and a six-month operating pilot with explicit approval gates before the first real referral.

Population

Discharged home without reliable support

Potential focus on older adults, people living alone, or patients facing multiple practical barriers.

Safety

Workflow + privacy approved first

Referral, escalation, consent, data access, insurance, supervision, and incident response settled before launch.

Workforce

Screened, trained Navigators

Structured preparation, clear role boundaries, quality review, and supervisor oversight.

Duration

About 30 days per patient

Resolution-based graduation, with unresolved work escalated or handed to a sustainable next owner.
