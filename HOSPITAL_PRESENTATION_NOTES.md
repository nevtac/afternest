# Hospital presentation pass

This pass reframes the prototype for a serious hospital partner conversation while preserving its academic/proposed status.

## Presentation goals

- Lead with the operating gap, not a student-project label.
- Position AfterNest as a non-clinical recovery execution layer that complements existing care-transition teams.
- Make hospital ownership versus AfterNest ownership explicit.
- Show approval gates before any real patient use.
- Separate practical process outcomes from clinical outcomes.
- Make the operations workspace, scorecard, governance, and partner brief screenshot-ready.
- Keep UCSF Health described only as a proposed target partner.

## Primary presentation pages

1. `/` - executive homepage
2. `/partners.html#pilot` - hospital pilot model
3. `/workspace.html` - illustrative operations environment
4. `/impact.html` - pilot scorecard
5. `/team.html` - governance and leadership model
6. `/partner-brief.html` - printable executive brief

## Safety posture

This remains a prototype. It does not accept real PHI, does not represent a confirmed hospital relationship, and does not claim clinical outcomes. A live pilot would require partner-approved legal, privacy, security, consent, workforce, insurance, escalation, data, and governance design.
