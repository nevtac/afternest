Six-month pilot roadmap

Build the organization, then prove the workflow.

The project lifecycle moves from nonprofit and governance foundations through partner design, Navigator recruitment, a focused soft launch, measurement, and formal pilot closeout.

Design principle

Do not scale to additional hospitals until the first pilot establishes a workable referral, training, supervision, privacy, escalation, and measurement model.

Month 0-1

Governance & legal foundation

Form the nonprofit corporation, define board experience profiles, draft bylaws, pursue 501(c)(3) recognition, establish insurance needs, and begin legal/privacy review of the proposed service.

Month 1-2

Hospital workflow design

Select one anchor hospital target, map the discharge/referral workflow, define eligibility, design patient opt-in and information-sharing procedures, and establish clinical escalation boundaries.

Month 2-3

Navigator program build

Recruit and screen the first Navigator cohort, finalize training modules, create supervisor procedures, verify language capabilities, and build the approved community-resource directory.

Month 3

Operational readiness test

Run tabletop scenarios from referral through graduation, test privacy boundaries, simulate escalation, verify dashboard workflows, and resolve gaps before patient enrollment.

Month 4

Soft launch

Begin with a very small number of eligible patients from one hospital location. Review every case closely and adjust SOPs, staffing, resources, and handoff procedures.

Month 4-6

Focused pilot operation

Each enrolled patient receives approximately 30 days of Navigator support while the program tracks barriers, resolution, escalation, patient experience, and operating feasibility.

Month 6

Pilot closeout & decision

Produce the outcome report, partner feedback summary, cost review, lessons learned, risk review, technology requirements, and recommendation to refine, expand, or stop.

Stage gates

What must be true before moving forward.

Before recruitment

Role scope, screening requirements, supervision model, privacy expectations, and training curriculum approved.

Before patient contact

Partner workflow, consent process, escalation pathway, approved resources, insurance, and operating procedures ready.

Before expansion

Pilot workflow is stable, core process metrics are measurable, risks are understood, and partner review supports the next phase.
