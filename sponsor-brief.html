Sponsor brief
AfterNest · Academic Prototype

Support the practical side of recovery.

AfterNest is a proposed nonprofit recovery support program designed for vulnerable patients who may understand what comes next after discharge but still lack the practical support to carry it out.

The model

One trained Recovery Navigator. One personalized non-clinical support plan. Every approved practical barrier has an owner and a visible status until it is resolved, declined, escalated, or formally handed off.

Why support is needed

The pilot depends on more than volunteer goodwill. A responsible program requires governance, screening, training, supervision, legal and privacy review, insurance, hospital integration, resource partnerships, technology, and evaluation.

What support enables

  • Nonprofit formation and governance
  • Hospital partnership development
  • Navigator recruitment, screening, training, and supervision
  • Transportation and community-resource coordination
  • Multilingual and accessibility-focused program design
  • Secure workflow and reporting infrastructure
  • Pilot evaluation and formal closeout reporting

Illustrative planning categories

The class proposal currently uses planning categories rather than a final audited budget: program staff and supervision, Navigator training and screening, legal and insurance review, transportation and resource support, technology and operations, and evaluation and reporting.

How impact would be reported

Sponsors would receive process-focused reporting that distinguishes activity from execution: patients enrolled, practical barriers identified, tasks resolved, escalation and handoff volume, time to resolution, patient experience, Navigator capacity, and pilot cost. Clinical outcome claims would not be made without appropriate evidence.

What success looks like after six months

A partner-reviewed pilot report showing whether AfterNest can recruit and supervise Navigators, integrate into one discharge workflow, support a clearly defined patient population, resolve practical barriers consistently, operate within appropriate boundaries, and justify a next phase.

Academic planning document only. No sponsorship, tax-exempt status, partnership, or audited budget currently exists.
