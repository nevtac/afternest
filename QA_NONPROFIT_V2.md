# AfterNest nonprofit UI refinement QA

Scope: homepage, global visual system, brand, navigation, volunteer experience, program page, hospital partner page, team/governance page, and operations brand alignment.

## Design audit applied
- Reduced oversized display type and tightened section spacing.
- Increased body readability and button tap targets.
- Replaced software-style violet gradients with a hospital/nonprofit palette: deep navy, evergreen teal, sage, warm sand, white.
- Simplified top navigation around service audiences: How it works, Who we serve, Volunteer, For hospitals, Our team.
- Made volunteering the primary public call to action.
- Shortened the homepage from a long product narrative to five focused sections.
- Replaced the homepage software dashboard with an illustrative patient story and service workflow.
- Added a new house-and-nest brand mark and matching favicon.
- Preserved the existing demo intake, Navigator workspace, patient plan, impact, training, resources, and printable partner materials.

## Content guardrails
- AfterNest remains described as a proposed nonprofit / academic pilot.
- No hospital partnership, 501(c)(3) status, clinical outcome, or production service is represented as confirmed.
- Recovery Navigator work remains explicitly non-clinical.
- Medical questions remain with licensed healthcare teams.

## Functional review
- Existing internal routes used by the new navigation are already present in the repository.
- Existing localStorage demo data keys and workflow functions are unchanged.
- Volunteer and partner form IDs / data attributes used by the existing JavaScript are preserved.
- Intake and workspace functionality are not removed.
