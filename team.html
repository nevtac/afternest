Proposed nonprofit leadership

Roles to recruit before a real pilot.

Instead of inventing named directors, the proposal defines the experience profiles AfterNest would need to operate responsibly.

Governance

Board of Directors

Independent oversight, mission protection, financial accountability, and executive supervision.

  • Healthcare/community perspective
  • Business or nonprofit governance
  • Finance or fundraising experience
Executive

Executive Director

Owns strategy, partner relationships, fundraising, board reporting, and overall program accountability.

  • Nonprofit or healthcare leadership
  • Partnership development
  • Operational decision-making
Clinical interface

Medical Social Work Advisor

Helps design referral boundaries, social-resource coordination, escalation pathways, and patient-support standards.

  • Licensed social work background preferred
  • Hospital transition experience
  • Community resource expertise
Operations

Director of Operations

Owns SOPs, referral workflow, Recovery Support Plan process, quality review, and pilot execution.

  • Program operations
  • Process design
  • Cross-functional coordination
People

Volunteer Manager

Recruits, screens, trains, schedules, supervises, and supports Recovery Navigators.

  • Volunteer management
  • Training and coaching
  • Escalation discipline
Risk & compliance

Legal / Privacy Lead

Reviews consent, information-sharing, insurance, volunteer scope, transportation, privacy, and regulatory requirements.

  • Healthcare privacy experience
  • California nonprofit knowledge
  • Risk management
Funding

Finance & Development Lead

Builds budgets, grant strategy, donor reporting, internal controls, and diversified funding.

  • Nonprofit finance
  • Grant development
  • Fundraising operations
Community

Partnerships & Communications Lead

Builds hospital, university, community-resource, sponsor, and volunteer-recruitment relationships.

  • Community engagement
  • Clear public messaging
  • Partner stewardship
Technology

Product / Systems Lead

Maintains the referral, Navigator, resource, reporting, and privacy-safe technology stack.

  • Secure workflow design
  • Accessibility
  • Data minimization
Governance deliverable

Board recruitment should be role-first, not name-first.

Before recruiting individuals, AfterNest would define the capabilities the board must collectively cover: healthcare transitions, community health, nonprofit governance, finance, legal/privacy, fundraising, and lived patient/caregiver perspective.
