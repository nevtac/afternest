Recovery Navigators

Be the person who helps the next step actually happen.

AfterNest Recovery Navigators provide structured, non-clinical support after discharge. The role is built around practical problem-solving, follow-through, boundaries, and respectful communication.

Potential recruitment pipeline

The proposal may recruit community volunteers and students in pre-health, nursing, public health, social work, or medical programs. This is a proposed model, not a confirmed university placement program.

The role

A Navigator is not there to give advice. They are there to make support usable.

The most important skill is reliable follow-through. A Navigator helps the patient work through practical barriers, confirms what happened, documents the non-clinical task, and escalates anything outside the role.

What Navigators do

Support without practicing medicine.

Coordinate

Help patients work through transportation, scheduling logistics, community resources, caregiver coordination, and basic-needs support.

Navigate

Help with portals, video visits, digital tools, non-clinical language navigation, and finding the right next resource.

Escalate

Recognize when a question is clinical and return it to the approved healthcare team instead of attempting to answer it.

Navigator readiness

Training is part of the service, not an afterthought.

Before being matched with a patient, each Navigator would complete a structured training pathway and demonstrate understanding of scope and escalation.
