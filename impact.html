Monthly

Month 6

Pilot closeout report

Summarize feasibility, process outcomes, patient and partner feedback, costs, lessons learned, and go/no-go recommendations for expansion.

Evidence discipline

Do not overclaim what the pilot proves.

AfterNest can measure whether practical tasks were completed and whether patients and partners found the service useful. Readmission reduction or other clinical outcomes would require appropriate study design, sufficient sample size, and partner-approved analysis before making causal claims.
